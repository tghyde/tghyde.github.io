Math 221 Linear Algebra

Vassar College · Fall 2026 · Two sections

Linear algebra, the art and science of solving linear equations, is the single most useful mathematical subject. We study vector spaces and linear transformations through geometry and algebra, by hand and by machine.

Course page

Math 301 Representation Theory

Senior Seminar · Vassar College · Fall 2026

Representation theory breaks symmetry down into its most fundamental, atomic pieces. In this inquiry-based seminar, students develop the theory for finite groups from scratch through problem solving, discussion, and presentations.
