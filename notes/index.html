Down the Rabbit Hole: An Introduction to p-adic Numbers

Math Day keynote address, Colorado State University · November 2026

Starting from the puzzle of why 0.999… = 1, the talk asks what happens if the digits run off to the left instead, works out how to add, subtract, and multiply such numbers, and arrives at Hensel’s p-adic numbers: an infinite family of number systems that sit alongside the real numbers. Along the way we meet zero divisors, square roots of −1, and the p-adic prism.
