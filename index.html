Trevor Hyde

Assistant Professor of Mathematics and Statistics
Vassar College

I work in arithmetic dynamics and number theory, with excursions into algebraic geometry, combinatorics, representation theory, and topology. Recurring themes in my work are the arithmetic of iteration, the statistics of polynomial factorization over finite fields and their connection to the cohomology of configuration spaces, and explicit Galois theory. Much of my recent work is collaborative, including with undergraduate coauthors.

I received my Ph.D. from the University of Michigan in 2019, advised by Jeffrey Lagarias and Michael Zieve, and my B.A. from Amherst College in 2012. Before coming to Vassar in 2023 I was an NSF Postdoctoral Fellow and L. E. Dickson Instructor at the University of Chicago.
